MASTERS Zagreb — Underground Electronic Music Club & Terrace

Masters Zagreb is an authentic underground electronic music club and covered summer terrace located at Sportski park Ravnice (Ravnice bb, 10000 Zagreb, Croatia). Established in 2007, Masters is renowned for pure vinyl DJ sets, custom audiophile sound systems, handcrafted wooden club aesthetics, and weekly underground house, techno, minimal, and electro nights.
